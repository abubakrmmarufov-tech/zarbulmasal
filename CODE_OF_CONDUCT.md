# Code of conduct

Zarbulmasal is a cultural and learning project. Everyone participating in its
issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions is expected to help create a
respectful and useful space.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Respect differences in language, script, culture, identity, and experience.
- Correct the work without attacking the person who made it.
- Cite credible sources when discussing linguistic or cultural accuracy.
- Accept good-faith feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized
content, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [@imarufov](https://t.me/imarufov) on
Telegram. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project.
