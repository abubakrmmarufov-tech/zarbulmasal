## 2024-05-27 - [Isolate Usage in Repository]
**Learning:** For large JSON files in Flutter, synchronous decoding causes main thread UI jank. `LiteratureRepository` already caches `_worksFuture` and uses `compute` for `works.json`. However, `poets.json` is also fairly large (~402K), and it is being decoded synchronously each time `loadAuthors()` is called, and `getAuthorById` also calls `loadAuthors()` repeatedly. Similarly `loadAuthors()` does not cache the result. `HistoryRepository` also has synchronous decodes but `entries.json` is ~172K.
**Action:** Caching the parsed result of `loadAuthors()` in `LiteratureRepository` (similar to how `loadWorks()` is cached) will avoid redundant decoding of a 402K file, significantly improving performance when navigating author pages or looking up authors. Offloading it to `compute` could also help but caching the `Future` is more effective. Let's cache `loadAuthors` as it's the second largest asset and accessed frequently.
