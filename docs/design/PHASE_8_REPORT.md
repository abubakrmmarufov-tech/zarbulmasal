## 1. Analysis first

- `docs/literature/POET_COVERAGE_2026-09-26.md`: for every poet, the chapter pages, the verse blocks on them, readable poems, needsReview records with the reason each is not readable, and a verdict (a/b/c/d), before any change. Generated by the new `tool/literature/poet_coverage.py`; the verdicts are mine, from reading the chapters, in `POET_COVERAGE_VERDICTS_2026-09-26.json`.
- **Why 4,981 records were not readable:** 3,651 are the automatic dump's candidates whose «title» is a fragment of prose not printed as verse; 463 open a single bayt; 449 are a prose line; 194 are title-only records of another edition (2020) with no PDF; 101 duplicate a published poem. Only a few dozen pointed at real, unpublished verse.
- **What the reading found:**
  - **Verse set flush left.** On some pages the text layer gives the verse no indent, so the Phase 7 extractor skipped it — and cut poems at those page breaks.
  - **Survey chapters** quote poets by name or sign the verse with their name. Phase 7 read them as the chapter of whoever's biography cites the page. It rejected verse the book gives, by name, to someone else, and filed one signed quatrain under the wrong poet.
  - **Biographies that cite the wrong pages:** Дақиқӣ (pp. 77–80 are Фирдавсӣ's; his chapter is 65–74), Амир Хусрав (p. 87 only; the chapter is 87–106), Гулчеҳра Сулаймонӣ (5:248–255 are other writers' chapters).
- `docs/literature/POEM_EXPANSION_PLAN_2026-09-26.md`: the plan, tool changes, expected counts and risks.

## 2. Tools

| Tool | Change |
|---|---|
| `textbook_span.py` (new) | `take_span` copies the printed lines between a named opening and closing line, across up to 8 pages. It skips page-foot footnotes and keeps «***» as a stanza break. It refuses headings, glossaries, questions, labels and prose-length lines. `after` handles a closing refrain; `lenient` lets through dashed verse lines on flush-left pages. `fix_lookalikes` sets Latin look-alike letters as the Cyrillic ones the page shows. |
| `extract_textbook_poems.load_pages` | Applies the look-alike fix to every page, so all tools and the audit see the letters the page shows. |
| `publish_reviewed_blocks.py` | New options: spans, `split: quatrains`, `heading`, `replaces` (repair a record that held part of a poem) and `merges` (a shorter excerpt reprinted in another book). It promotes a matching needsReview record instead of creating a new one, and retires its duplicates in the catalogue's conventions. It writes `recordIds` back to the log. |
| `audit_textbook_poems.py` | Reads every review log and judges prose per page. **New check:** the line under a poem must not sign it with another poet's name. |
| `poet_coverage.py`, `fix_lookalike_letters.py` (new) | The coverage report, and the look-alike fix for kept records (logged). |

All new behaviour has tests: `test_textbook_span.py`, `test_poet_coverage.py`, `test_fix_lookalike_letters.py`, and new cases in the publisher and audit tests.

## 5. App health

**Crash safety.**

- The API 24 emulator was **not run**. I installed the Android 7.0 arm64 system image and made a 2 GB AVD (`zarb_api24`), but the emulator will not start: it needs 7.4 GB free, and the Mac has 2.2 GB (the disk is 99% full).
- **On the phone** (Xiaomi 2412DPC0AG, Android 16, profile build):
  - no crash and no ANR;
  - the crash log has no entries for the app.

**Cold start** (`am start -W`, profile, 5 runs): 1.63 s on the first run after install, then 1.11, 0.87, 0.75, 0.72 s.

- **No frame reached 700 ms.** The worst was search's first keystroke, at 537 ms.
- **Cause:** search re-normalised every field of both items inside the sort comparator, which is O(n log n) normalisations per keystroke, and a one-letter query matches most of the catalogue.
- **Fixed (`2437c54`):** each match is scored once, then sorted, with ties in catalogue order. A test covers the ranking.
- **Not re-measured on the phone** (see the incident below). On a slow Android 7 phone the old code could have crossed 700 ms.
