## 1. Executive Summary & Selection Methodology

The inaugural cohort of the **Мероси адабӣ (Literary Heritage)** module comprises ten landmark poets representing the historical continuum of Tajik literature across eleven centuries.

### Selection Criteria
1. **Curricular Centrality**: Poets systematically mandated in the state secondary school curriculum approved by the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Tajikistan (*Хониши адабӣ* for primary grades, *Адабиёти тоҷик* for secondary grades 5–11).
2. **National & Historical Significance**: Figures recognized with the highest national honors (Қаҳрамони Тоҷикистон, Шоири халқии Тоҷикистон, Lenin/State Prizes, State Anthem authorship, and national commemorative days).
3. **Philological Availability**: Availability of verified Tier A critical editions published by reputable academic presses (Адиб, Дониш, Ирфон, Ношир).
4. **Balanced Epochal Representation**:
   - Classical / Golden Age (9th–14th centuries): Foundation of classical Persian-Tajik poetic forms (qasida, ghazal, rubai, masnavi).
   - 20th-Century Soviet Era: Classical revival, patriotic poetry, epic narratives, social transformation.
   - Late 20th–21st-Century Independence Era: National sovereignty, lyrical depth, philosophical introspection, and modern free verse (*шеъри нав*).
   - Female Literary Heritage: Leading female voices of modern Tajik letters.

## 3. Detailed Poet Dossiers

### 1. Абӯабдуллоҳи Рӯдакӣ (Abu Abdullah Rudaki)
- **PERIOD**: 9th–10th century (Samanid Golden Age; c. 858 – c. 940/941 CE).
- **WHY IMPORTANT**: Widely venerated as *"Одамушшуаро"* (Father of Poets) and *"Султони шоирон"* (Sultan of Poets). Founder and architect of Persian-Tajik literature and pioneer of the classical poetic canon (ghazal, qasida, rubai, masnavi). His birth anniversary, **September 22**, is celebrated nationwide by presidential decree as official **Rudaki Day** (*Рӯзи Рӯдакӣ*).
- **OFFICIAL EDUCATIONAL EVIDENCE**: A cornerstone of national education across all tiers:
  - Grade 4 (*Хониши адабӣ*): Primary ethical rubaiyat and educational bayts.
  - Grade 5 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Rudaki's life, Samanid court period, and foundational poems (*«Бӯи Ҷӯи Мӯлиён»*).
  - Grade 8 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Classical lyrical style, panegyric and philosophical qasidas.
  - Grade 10 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): In-depth literary theory, metre (*aruz*), and the origins of modern Tajik literature.
- **MAJOR EDITIONS**:
  - *Осори Рӯдакӣ*, ed. Abdulghani Mirzoev (Stalinabad: Nashriyoti Davlatii Tojikiston, 1958).
  - *Ашъори Рӯдакӣ* (Силсилаи *«Ахтарони адаб»*, Vol. 1; Dushanbe: Adib, 2008).
  - *Девони Рӯдакӣ*, ed. Qodir Rustam (Dushanbe: Adib, 2012 / 2nd edition 2015).
- **COPYRIGHT**: `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` (Deceased c. 940 CE; >1,080 years post mortem).
- **FULL TEXT ELIGIBLE**: **YES** (Subject to dual-source line verification).
- **DECISION**: **INCLUDE** as Tier 1 Classical Anchor.

### 2. Носири Хусрав (Nasir Khusraw)
- **PERIOD**: 11th century (1004 – c. 1077–1088 CE).
- **WHY IMPORTANT**: Master philosopher, poet, traveller, and theologian (*Ҳуҷҷати Хуросон*). Author of the celebrated travelogue *Сафарнома (Safarnama)*, didactic masnavis (*Рӯшноинома*, *Саодатнома*), and the massive philosophical *Девони ашъор*. Pioneer of philosophical and ethical rationalism in Tajik verse.
- **OFFICIAL EDUCATIONAL EVIDENCE**:
  - Grade 8 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Selections from *Сафарнома* and ethical qasidas.
  - Grade 10 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Didactic poetry, allegorical tales, and philosophical qasidas emphasizing knowledge, reason, and social ethics.
- **MAJOR EDITIONS**:
  - *Девони ашъор* (Силсилаи *«Ахтарони адаб»*, Vol. 6; Dushanbe: Adib, 2010).
  - *Сафарнома* (Dushanbe: Irfon, 1970; Adib, 2003).
  - *Рӯшноинома* and *Саодатнома* (Academy editions; Dushanbe: Donish).
- **COPYRIGHT**: `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` (Deceased c. 1077–1088 CE; >930 years post mortem).
- **FULL TEXT ELIGIBLE**: **YES** (Subject to dual-source line verification).
- **DECISION**: **INCLUDE** as Classical Didactic & Philosophical Anchor.

### 3. Камоли Хуҷандӣ (Kamol Khujandi)
- **PERIOD**: 14th century (Timurid / Post-Mongol era; c. 1320 – c. 1400 CE).
- **WHY IMPORTANT**: Supreme master of the lyrical ghazal, traditionally paired in Persian literary history with Hafez Shirazi (*«Шеъри Камол оби ҳаёт аст»*). In 2020, his 700th birth jubilee was officially celebrated at the state level with the patronage and backing of UNESCO.
- **OFFICIAL EDUCATIONAL EVIDENCE**:
  - Grade 8 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Introduction to Kamol's biography, exile in Tabriz, and love for his homeland Khujand.
  - Grade 10 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Detailed analysis of ghazal structure, delicate imagery, and subtle wordplay (*содагӣ ва саҳли мумтанеъ*).
- **MAJOR EDITIONS**:
  - *Девон* (Dushanbe: Irfon, 1983).
  - *Девон*, ed. S. Siddiqov (Dushanbe: Adib, 2015).
  - *Девон* (Jubilee Edition in 2 volumes; Khujand: Noshir, 2020).
- **COPYRIGHT**: `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` (Deceased c. 1400 CE; >620 years post mortem).
- **FULL TEXT ELIGIBLE**: **YES** (Subject to dual-source line verification).
- **DECISION**: **INCLUDE** as Classical Lyrical Ghazal Anchor.

### 4. Мирзо Турсунзода (Mirzo Tursunzoda)
- **PERIOD**: 20th century (Soviet Tajik Era; May 2 [April 19], 1911 – September 24, 1977).
- **WHY IMPORTANT**: Hero of Tajikistan (*Қаҳрамони Тоҷикистон*, 2001 posthumous), Chairman of the Union of Writers of Tajikistan (1946–1977), Lenin Prize laureate (1960), USSR State Prize laureate. His portrait is honored on the 1 Somoni national banknote. Author of timeless civic poems and epic dāstāns (*«Садои Осиё»*, *«Ҷони ширин»*, *«Ҳасани аробакаш»*).
- **OFFICIAL EDUCATIONAL EVIDENCE**:
  - Grade 4 (*Хониши адабӣ*): Patriotic verses on the Motherland and friendship.
  - Grade 7 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Civic lyrics, *«Садои Осиё»*, *«Қиссаи Ҳиндустон»*.
  - Grade 11 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Comprehensive monographic study of Soviet Tajik literature, poetic drama, and lyrical epics.
- **MAJOR EDITIONS**:
  - *Куллиёт* in 4 volumes (Dushanbe: Irfon, 1971–1985).
  - *Осори мунтахаб* in 2 volumes (Dushanbe: Adib, 1989).
  - *Куллиёт* in 6 volumes (Dushanbe: Donish, 2025 jubilee edition).

### 5. Муъмин Қаноат (Mumin Qanoat)
- **PERIOD**: 20th–21st century (May 5, 1932 – May 18, 2018).
- **WHY IMPORTANT**: People's Poet of Tajikistan (*Шоири халқии Тоҷикистон*, 1991), USSR State Prize laureate (1977), Chairman of the Union of Writers of Tajikistan (1977–1991). ### 6. Лоиқ Шералӣ (Loiq Sherali)
- **PERIOD**: Late 20th century (May 20, 1941 – June 30, 2000).
- **WHY IMPORTANT**: People's Poet of Tajikistan (*Шоири халқии Тоҷикистон*, 1991), Rudaki State Prize laureate. Unquestionably the most beloved modern lyrical poet in Tajikistan and across Persian-speaking world. Architect of modern national awakening, romantic lyricism, and modern rubaiyat (*«Модарнома»*, *«Сад барги ғазал»*, *«Дасти дуои модар»*).
- **OFFICIAL EDUCATIONAL EVIDENCE**:

  - Grade 7 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Native land and mother tongue poetry (*«Забони модарӣ»*).
  - Grade 9 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Lyrical ghazals and civic rubaiyat.
  - Grade 11 (*Адабиёти тоҷик*): Deep monographic study of modern lyricism, renaissance of classical forms, and national identity.
- **MAJOR EDITIONS**:

### 10. Фарзона (Фарзонаи Хуҷандӣ / Иноят Хоҷаева)

## 4. Cohort Roadmap & Next Expansions

Following complete verification of the foundation cohort of 10 poets, future expansions will evaluate the following curriculum figures under the same criteria:
- **Classical Wave 2**: Саъдии Шерозӣ (Saadi Shirazi), Ҳофизи Шерозӣ (Hafez Shirazi), Мавлоно Ҷалолуддини Балхӣ (Rumi), Абдурраҳмони Ҷомӣ (Jami).
- **Enlightenment / 19th c. Wave**: Аҳмади Дониш (Ahmad Donish), Шамсиддин Шоҳин (Shamsiddin Shohin).
- **Early 20th c. National Awakening**: Садриддин Айнӣ (Sadriddin Ayni), Абулқосим Лоҳутӣ (Abulqosim Lohuti), Пайрав Сулаймонӣ (Pairav Sulaymoni).
