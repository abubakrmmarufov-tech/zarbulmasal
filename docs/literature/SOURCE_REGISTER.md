## 1. Registry Overview

The **Source Register** records bibliographic leads and historical editorial claims for the Zarbulmasal Literary Heritage module. It is not, by itself, proof that a listed edition was accessed or that a claim is ready for publication.

**Evidence boundary — 2026-09-20:** In the current checkout, a source is a
checked witness only when its file is present under `docs/literature/pdfs/` or
its approved `maorif.tj` URL is recorded in the active data, its printed page
has been inspected, and the corresponding local page-image/provenance checks
pass. Rows below that say `CONSULTED`, `ACTIVE`, or `VERIFIED` are legacy
claims unless they meet that boundary; they must not be used as release
evidence or to promote a work. Current release provenance is governed by
`docs/literature/POEM_PAGE_PROOF.md`, the active JSON `sourceReference` fields,
and the strict provenance linter.

All entries are classified according to the Source Hierarchy established in `SOURCE_POLICY.md` (Tier A, Tier B, Tier C).

## 5. Maintenance Procedures

1. Every new printed volume obtained or consulted must be appended to this register prior to citing its page numbers in JSON database records.
2. If an edition ceases to meet Tier A or Tier B criteria (e.g., unauthorized reprint with unverified edits), its status must be altered to `REJECTED` and recorded in `REJECTED_MATERIAL.md`.
3. Year and editor columns must match physical title page imprints exactly.
