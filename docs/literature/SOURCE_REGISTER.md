## 1. Registry Overview

The **Source Register** maintains a comprehensive, audit-ready record of all printed editions, scholarly series, institutional repositories, and digital facsimiles consulted for the Zarbulmasal Literary Heritage module.

All entries are classified according to the Source Hierarchy established in `SOURCE_POLICY.md` (Tier A, Tier B, Tier C).

## 5. Maintenance Procedures

1. Every new printed volume obtained or consulted must be appended to this register prior to citing its page numbers in JSON database records.
2. If an edition ceases to meet Tier A or Tier B criteria (e.g., unauthorized reprint with unverified edits), its status must be altered to `REJECTED` and recorded in `REJECTED_MATERIAL.md`.
3. Year and editor columns must match physical title page imprints exactly.
