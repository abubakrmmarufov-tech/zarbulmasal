## How to read the table

- **Chapter pages:** the pages the poet's biography cites, plus the chapter the book heads with the poet's name (and dates), up to the next such heading. Where a biography cites the wrong pages the verdict note says so (Дақиқӣ, Амир Хусрав, Гулчеҳра Сулаймонӣ).
- **Verse blocks:** runs of four or more lines on those pages that read as verse, indented or set flush left; in brackets, how many were not yet published. A block is not always the poet's: survey chapters quote many poets, and the count includes quotations of others.
- **needsReview: why not readable:** each needsReview record, grouped by the first reason it cannot be read.
- **Verdict:** **a** verse exists and can be published; **b** verse exists but is not the poet's (folk verse, other poets, translations, examples); **c** the chapter prints no publishable verse (a prose writer, or only a lone bayt — the app never publishes a lone bayt as a work); **d** no chapter in these books.

### What the reading found beyond the table

- **Verse set flush left.** The text layer gives some pages' verse no indent; the Phase 7 extractor skips it. On those pages it also cut poems at a page break (Халилӣ's «Хитоб ба авлоди Ватан», Биноӣ's «Чоҳкан зери чоҳ» and «Сари Ҳотам», Ҳилолӣ's «Дар бораи одоб», Аминзода's and Шукӯҳӣ's poems, Ғаффор Мирзо's «Дӯст»).
- **Survey chapters** (grade 8, pp. 29–48 and 144–149; grade 11, pp. 14–21 and 338–345) quote many poets by name or with a signature under the verse. Phase 7 read them only as the chapter of whoever's biography cites the page, so it rejected verse that the book gives, by name, to another poet — and filed one signed quatrain under the wrong poet (Шаҳиди Балхӣ's, under Абушакур).
- **Verse under a signature line** «(Кисоии Марвазӣ)» and after «***» separators was not found at all.
- **Latin look-alike letters** in 13 records (14 words).
